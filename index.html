🎯 What is TiviIPTVPlayer?

📋 System Basics

While TiviIPTVPlayer is very flexible, here's what we recommend for the best experience:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 version 1809 or newer, or Windows 11 (all versions).
  • Internet Connection: A stable broadband connection (at least 10 Mbps for standard HD streams, more for 4K).
  • RAM: 4 GB or more is ideal, though it works with less.
  • Storage: App itself takes less than 100 MB of space. Your playlist and EPG data will take a few additional MB.

Don't worry about specifics—if you can run Windows, you can run this app.

🔥 Tips for the Best Viewing Experience

  • Use a wired Ethernet connection for buffering-free streaming, especially for 4K channels.
  • Update your graphics drivers through Windows Update to avoid video glitches.
  • If channels don't load, check your internet connection and try a different network (like your phone's hotspot) to see if the issue is with your ISP.
  • Keep your playlist file updated. If a channel stops working, contact your provider for a refreshed link.
